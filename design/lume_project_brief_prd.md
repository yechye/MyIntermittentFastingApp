# Project Brief: Lume Fasting App

## 1. Project Overview
Lume is a premium, iOS-native intermittent fasting tracker designed to simplify the fasting journey through a clean, wellness-focused interface. The app moves away from traditional, high-pressure countdown timers in favor of a chronological "narrative" approach that provides users with better context of their progress within their daily cycle.

## 2. Brand Identity
*   **Brand Name:** Lume (formerly Vitality)
*   **Primary Brand Color:** Vitality Sage (`#7FB069`) - represents growth, health, and vitality.
*   **Accent Color:** Sun Gold - used for secondary status indicators and sun-related iconography.
*   **Typography:** Hanken Grotesk - a clean, modern sans-serif that balances approachability with clarity.
*   **Visual Style:** Minimalist, iOS-native aesthetics, high contrast light/dark modes, and soft surface elevations.

## 3. Core Features & Requirements
### 3.1. Narrative Timer & Timeline
*   **Vertical Timeline:** A top-to-bottom chronological view of the fasting journey.
*   **Dynamic Progress Node:** The elapsed time is displayed as a prominent node within the timeline, moving vertically as the fast progresses.
*   **Milestones:** Clear indicators for "Fasting Started," "Current Progress," and "Window Opens."
*   **Interactions:** Capability to edit the start time and end the fast early via a primary CTA.

### 3.2. Daily Metrics & Insights
*   **Streak Tracking:** Visual counter for consecutive successful fasts.
*   **Hydration Log:** Quick-view and logging for daily water intake.
*   **Phase Indicators:** Contextual labels for metabolic states (e.g., "Burning Fat," "Ketosis Stage").

### 3.3. Experience Modes
*   **Sage Light Mode:** High-energy, clean daytime interface.
*   **Dark Mode:** Premium, low-strain charcoal-navy interface for evening use.

## 4. User Experience Goals
*   **Clarity:** Provide an immediate, at-a-glance understanding of "Where am I now?" and "When do I eat next?"
*   **Calmness:** Use soft colors and generous whitespace to reduce the anxiety often associated with weight management tools.
*   **Native Feel:** Adhere strictly to iOS Human Interface Guidelines for navigation, spacing, and haptic-friendly interactions.

## 5. Technical Specifications
*   **Platform:** iOS
*   **Language:** Swift / SwiftUI
*   **Design System:** Vitality Flux / Circadian Wellness (Sage-led)
